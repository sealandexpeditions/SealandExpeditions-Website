Cave Tubing

Minimum of 4 people per tour

Cave Tubing in Belize offers an exhilarating adventure, taking you on a journey under the lush forest canopy, down the meandering Caves Branch river, and into the fascinating cave passages of the Nohoch Che'en Archaeological Reserve. Located just a 45-minute drive from Belize City or a 1-hour drive from San Ignacio at mile 37 on the George Price Highway, this experience promises excitement from the moment you arrive.

Upon arrival, you'll have the chance to change and freshen up in the changing rooms and restrooms. Then, equipped with helmets, headlights, and personal flotation devices (PFDs), all safety equipment is mandatory for everyone taking part in the tour. After gearing up you'll embark on a guided hike through the tropical broadleaf forest.

Your friendly tour guide will share intriguing facts about the plants and wildlife that call this environment home. As you continue the leisurely hike, you'll eventually reach the entrance of the cave. From there, you'll embark on a 1.5-hour river tubing adventure on specially designed tubes, floating downstream through the cave passages. Along the way, your guide will provide insights into the geological wonders and ancient history of the limestone caves. Keep in mind that Cave Tubing is a water activity, so prepare to get wet.

Things to bring

Bug spray, sunscreen, dry clothes for the drive back after the tour, swimming trunks or a bathing suit. Footwear should have closed heels: water shoes, crocs, dive booties, or sandals are ideal. No flip flops allowed. If you don't have suitable footwear, rentals are available on-site.

Price

$65.00 USD per person

Price includes archaeological reserve fee, gear rental, and tour guide fees.

We also cater for transportation to and from wherever you are staying (hotel, airport, Airbnb), with lunch and complimentary drinks, for an added fee.

Xunantunich & Cave Tubing

Minimum of 3 people per tour

Embark on a mesmerizing Belizean adventure with our Xunantunich and Cave Tubing Combo Tour, a seamless blend of ancient Mayan wonders and exhilarating natural experiences. Your day begins with a comfortable transfer from your accommodations, setting the tone for a day filled with exploration and discovery.

First, we transport you to the mystical archaeological site of Xunantunich, where you'll step back in time and immerse yourself in the grandeur of Mayan history. Ascend the towering El Castillo, the main pyramid, and marvel at panoramic views of the lush Belizean landscape from its summit. Our expert guides, well-versed in Mayan history and culture, illuminate the secrets of this ancient city.

After a morning of cultural exploration, enjoy an authentic Belizean lunch featuring local ingredients and traditional recipes. The afternoon unfolds with an exhilarating cave tubing expedition at Nohoch Che'en Archaeological Reserve. Equip yourself with a helmet, headlamp, and personal flotation device (PFD), then hike to the cave entrance and float along the gentle currents of an underground river, passing through awe-inspiring cave systems adorned with mesmerizing formations. Our skilled guides ensure both safety and an informative narrative, sharing the geological and historical significance of the caves.

At the conclusion of your cave tubing exploration, we provide a convenient transfer back to your accommodation. Throughout the day, Sealand Expeditions guarantees not just a tour but a seamless and immersive experience, leaving you with cherished memories of Belize's cultural and natural treasures.

Things to bring

A change of clothes, sunscreen, bug spray. Hiking shoes or sneakers are recommended for the Mayan temple; no open-heel shoes are allowed for cave tubing. Water shoes, dive booties, or crocs are ideal for this adventure.

Price

$210.00 USD per person

We also cater for transportation to and from wherever you are staying (hotel, airport, Airbnb), with lunch and complimentary drinks, for an added fee.
