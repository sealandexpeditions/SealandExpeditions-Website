FAQ

Some commonly asked questions about our services.

If you have any further questions or concerns, kindly contact us. We are ready to assist you!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour in advance?▼

To book a tour in advance you can visit our website and find information on available tours. Once you have chosen a tour, click the "Book Now" button, or contact us by email at sealandexpeditions@gmail.com or WhatsApp at (+501) 671-4571.

Are there age restrictions for certain tours?▼

Cave tubing and ziplining have an age restriction of 8 years old minimum or 48 inches tall.

Are meals provided during full day tours?▼

Yes! Meals and drinks are provided on all full day tours.

Do you accommodate meals with dietary restrictions?▼

Yes, we cater for dietary restrictions too!

What is the best way to get in touch in case of emergencies or last minute changes to the itinerary?▼

You can contact us by email at sealandexpeditions@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at (+501) 671-4571 for any inquiries.

Payment

What is the exchange rate of USD?▼

The exchange rate of USD to BZD is 1 to 2. 1 USD is equal to 2 BZD.

Can I pay in USD?▼

Yes, you can pay in USD. USD is widely accepted throughout the entire country.

Transfers

Are transfers included in tour packages?▼

Not all tour packages include transfers, however SeaLand Expeditions does cater for transfers. Feel free to ask us about transfer deals.
